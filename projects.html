SafeMed Automatic Pill Organizer
Arduino • Embedded Systems • Accessibility • Product Design

SafeMed is a functioning automatic pill organizer designed to help seniors manage medication schedules safely and independently.

The system uses embedded control logic, a keypad interface, and a physical dispensing mechanism to automate medication delivery and reduce user error.

Arduino Embedded Systems Circuit Design Hardware Prototyping
  • Built a full physical prototype with integrated electronics and enclosure design
  • Designed keypad-based input for simple user interaction
  • Connected mechanical movement with embedded control behavior
  • Focused on accessibility and medication safety for older adults

IBD Life Starter
Health Tech • Web Design • Data Organization • Patient Support

IBD Life Starter is a health-focused website concept designed to help people recently diagnosed with Crohn’s disease or ulcerative colitis adjust to daily life.

The project organizes trusted medical information and patient experience themes into simple guides about food, travel, symptoms, and adjustment after diagnosis. Future versions can connect scraped research data to a structured spreadsheet and display approved summaries on the website.

Health Tech Web Design IBD Support Data Organization Patient Resources
  • Designed as a resource page for people newly diagnosed with IBD
  • Separates trusted medical information from patient experience themes
  • Built into my portfolio with HTML, CSS, GitHub, and Cloudflare
  • Planned pipeline: scrape sources, store summaries in Excel, export approved content to the site

Drone Fertilizer Attachment Prototype
SkillsUSA • Arduino • Hardware Prototyping • Agricultural Innovation

This SkillsUSA project focused on a practical engineering solution for agriculture. I built a prototype attachment using Arduino circuitry and a physical model to show how a drone-based fertilizer spreading system could help farmers cover rough or hard-to-reach terrain.

The project strengthened my skills in electronics, prototyping, and turning an idea into a working proof of concept with a clear use case.

Arduino Circuit Design Hardware Prototyping Engineering Design Agriculture
  • Designed and assembled a prototype model for a drone-based fertilizer attachment
  • Integrated Arduino hardware, wiring, and battery-powered electronics
  • Built a physical structure to demonstrate the concept and component layout
  • Focused on a farming problem involving difficult terrain
  • Presented the concept through SkillsUSA competition work
