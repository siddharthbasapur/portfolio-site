IBD Life Starter

IBD Life Starter is a website concept for people recently diagnosed with Crohn’s disease or ulcerative colitis. The goal is to help users adjust to daily life through simple guides, trusted medical information, and clearly labeled patient experience themes.

Start Here

A new IBD diagnosis can feel confusing. This section is designed to give users a simple starting point before they explore food, symptoms, travel, school, work, and mental health topics.

What is IBD?

Inflammatory bowel disease, or IBD, is a term that mainly includes Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis. A new diagnosis can feel overwhelming, so learning the basics is an important first step.

Trusted medical information

Questions for your doctor

Newly diagnosed patients may want to ask what type of IBD they have, what treatment options exist, what symptoms to watch for, and when to seek urgent care.

Doctor visit guide

Adjustment takes time

Many people need time to adjust emotionally and practically after an IBD diagnosis. Food, symptoms, stress, routines, and support systems can all affect daily life.

Lifestyle support

Food & Eating

Food is one of the first lifestyle questions many people have after an IBD diagnosis. This section should avoid giving strict diet rules and instead explain that food triggers can vary from person to person.

Food triggers are personal

Trusted medical information

There is no single IBD diet that works for everyone. Some people notice that certain foods affect their symptoms, especially during flares. Tracking meals and symptoms can help identify patterns to discuss with a doctor or dietitian.

Eating during flares

Needs medical guidance

During symptom flare-ups, some people may need temporary food changes. The right approach depends on the person, symptoms, and nutrition needs.

Travel & Daily Life

This section focuses on practical lifestyle support. The goal is to make travel, school, work, and social life feel less overwhelming for people adjusting to IBD.

Plan before long trips

Traveling with IBD can feel easier when users plan ahead, pack extra medication, and think through possible food, bathroom, and symptom issues before leaving.

Lifestyle guide

Emergency kit idea

Many people with IBD say that carrying an emergency kit helps them feel more comfortable away from home. Common items include wipes, extra underwear, hand sanitizer, and plastic bags.

Patient experience theme

Bathroom anxiety

People with IBD may feel nervous about being far from a bathroom. This website could organize practical coping ideas while clearly separating patient experience from medical advice.

Daily life support

Research & Content Process

The long-term goal is to collect information from trusted medical sources, patient stories, articles, and public discussions, then turn that research into short, reviewed summaries for the website.

1. Collect trusted sources first

Medical foundation
Step 1
  • Use sources like Crohn’s & Colitis Foundation, Mayo Clinic, NHS, and Cleveland Clinic.
  • Use these sources for basic definitions, diagnosis support, treatment topics, nutrition, and doctor questions.

2. Summarize instead of copying

Ethical content use
Step 2
  • Scraped information should be used as research notes, not copied directly onto the website.
  • Each page should use original summaries, simple language, and links to original sources.

3. Label patient experience clearly

Safety and trust
Step 3
  • Reddit, blogs, and social media should be used for personal experience themes only.
  • Patient stories should not be presented as medical advice.

IBD Life Starter is an early prototype for a larger lifestyle resource. Future versions could include researched summaries, travel checklists, food and symptom tracking templates, and organized patient experience themes from public online sources.

This project shows web design, health-focused content organization, ethical research use, and user-centered thinking.
